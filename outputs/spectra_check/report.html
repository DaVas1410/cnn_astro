04 · Example spectra

Individual power spectra

Three representative images per database. Power is numerical noise (~10⁻¹⁶) below k_min, turns on sharply at the green line, follows the power law, and is band-limited at k_max (red).

Method. Band edges from the azimuthally-averaged 2D FFT power spectrum via band_edge_params (lower edge at 10% of peak, upper cutoff at 10⁻¹² of peak). Generation reused scripts/run_generation_flexible.py (fixed pyFC band-limiting). Verification: scripts/verify_spectra.py · plots: scripts/plot_distributions.py.

Context. This confirms end-to-end that the previously-reported pyFC “k_max not enforced” bug is resolved: k_max is now a real, causal band limit in freshly generated data.
